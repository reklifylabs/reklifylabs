## 💡 How We Work

Our approach is simple: understand the problem, build the right solution, and keep improving.

**01 — Understand**  
We identify the real problem, the intended audience, and what success should look like.

**02 — Design**  
We shape the idea into a clear scope and an intuitive user experience.

**03 — Build**  
We develop, test, and refine the solution with usability, performance, and maintainability in mind.

**04 — Improve**  
We launch, learn from real usage, and identify opportunities to make the product better.
